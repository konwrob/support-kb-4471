The three problems

1 · The parser reads day-first dates month first

Reports: 379, from 11 tenants. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: “Customer booked 7 April and got an invite for 4 July.” It reaches people through the app, by email, and through the booking-v2 form. This is the bug #4471 describes. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. The count follows the desk’s rule: it includes the reports read as a one-day move (problem 3), filed here by tenant. Ticket: #1.

2 · The booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY

Reports: 33, from 3 tenants, all through the booking form. These tenants run booking-v3, whose date field prints the hint MM/DD/YYYY. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The dates come out swapped just like problem 1, but the parser is not involved: the parser fix does not touch them. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so its hint can no longer swap dates. Ticket: #2.

3 · Dates move by one day

Reports: 5. Each describes a date that moves by exactly one day, not a swapped day and month: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json). By the desk’s rule they stay filed and counted under the tenant’s cause, problem 1, but the parser fix will not close them. A day-first parser cannot shift a date by one day; this looks like a time zone conversion. Still open after the parser fix. Ticket: #3.
